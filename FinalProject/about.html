About Simple ToDo

Simple ToDo is designed for students and young professionals who want to stay organized and motivated every day. Our mission is to help you focus on your goals and manage tasks in a simple, stylish, and stress-free way.

  • Mobile-first, clean, and modern design
  • Easy to use for all ages and backgrounds
  • All your tasks are safely stored in your browser
  • Free to use, no login needed

Let’s make every day productive and enjoyable!
